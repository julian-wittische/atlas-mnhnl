
## Description

Large size with a body length of 14 to 17mm. 

## Habitat
### Immature

Larvae live and develop in the wet wood of dead deciduous trees, stumps, and logs.

### Mature

Old beech and oak forests. Adults feed on many flowers including Apiaceae and flowering shrubs such as Cornus.

## Distribution
Widespread from France to the Baltic states and European Russia, but rarest of the Temnostoma species in Luxembourg.

## Notes

Temnostoma also mimi wasps in behavior with the extension of forelegs beyond their head to resemble wasp antennae.
